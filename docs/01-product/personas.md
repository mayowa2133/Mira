# Personas

## Primary — "Maya", the large-wardrobe owner

Mira is designed for her first. Every default should be the right default for
Maya.

**Characteristics**

- owns many clothes (150–500+ pieces)
- shops online and in stores
- frequently forgets what she owns
- has some unworn clothing
- may have garments with tags still attached
- owns shoes, bags and accessories in addition to clothes
- likes fashion; follows it visually rather than analytically
- dislikes manually cataloguing possessions
- often struggles to choose an outfit
- wants to know how outfits look before physically trying them on

**Things she actually says**

> "I have nothing to wear."
> "I forgot I even owned this."
> "I think I already have something like that."
> "What shoes go with this?"
> "I don't feel like trying everything on."
> "What would this look like on me?"

**What she will not tolerate**

- typing 300 garments into a form
- an app that looks like inventory or warehouse software
- being asked for a SKU
- an empty app that only becomes useful in three months
- a "closet" that is a spreadsheet with pictures

**What makes her come back**

- outfit ideas she would not have thought of, using clothes she owns
- rediscovering a piece she forgot about
- seeing an outfit on herself before committing to it

**Success for Maya**

She reaches 20+ confirmed owned items during initial setup, and later opens Mira
because she wants to, not because she needs to organize something.

## Secondary — "Nina", the intentional shopper

Owns 60–120 pieces, curates deliberately, cares about cost-per-wear and about not
buying duplicates. Uses Mira's duplicate detection and wardrobe intelligence more
than its stylist.

She matters because she stress-tests: purchase history accuracy, duplicate
detection precision, and cost-per-wear correctness.

## Secondary — "Alex", the outfit-repeater

Owns a moderate wardrobe and wears ~20% of it. The forgotten-pieces and
never-worn surfaces are the whole product for Alex. Try-on is the hook that gets
Alex to try a combination they would otherwise skip.

## Explicit non-persona for V1

- **The stylist / professional user** managing multiple clients' wardrobes.
  Multi-closet management is out of scope. See [non-goals](non-goals.md).
- **The reseller** cataloguing inventory to sell. Mira is not a marketplace.
